### Why Go?

Simply put, no other language cleanly fits the bill for building core utilities:

*   **C / C++:** Plagued by memory safety issues, burdened by archaic syntax, and weighed down by decades of legacy fluff.
*   **Rust:** Rust has the opposite problem. It modernized the toolchain but completely ignored the C-family lineage in its syntax, making it feel even more convoluted and archaic than C++ at times.
*   **Python & Java:** While syntactically cleaner, VM-reliant or interpreted languages simply aren't a fit for standalone system utilities.

This leaves **Go**. While it has its own fair share of quirks, it delivers the best balance of readable syntax, solid design, raw performance, and—crucially—simple, self-contained binaries.
